EMOTION WHEEL - QUESTIONS, RISKS, AND BALANCING TIPS
=====================================================

JOY
---
[joy-1-optimistic] Optimistic
Question: What possibilities do you see? What could go right in this situation?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to overlook real risks or appear naive.
Tip: To balance, consider: What realistic challenges exist? How can you prepare for potential obstacles?
Opposite: sad-2-depressed

[joy-2-confident] Confident
Question: What strengths can you bring to this situation? What are you capable of doing?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to appear arrogant or dismiss others' input.
Tip: To balance, consider: What could you learn from others? Where might you benefit from support or advice?
Opposite: fear-4-insecure

[joy-3-joyful] Joyful
Question: What brings you delight right now? What feels wonderful about this moment?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to ignore real problems or appear insensitive to others' struggles.
Tip: To balance, consider: Are there concerns that need attention? How can you honor both joy and challenges?
Opposite: sad-3-lonely

[joy-4-loving] Loving
Question: What do you appreciate about this person or situation? How can you show care and support?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to lose boundaries or neglect your own needs.
Tip: To balance, consider: What are your own needs right now? How can you care for yourself while caring for others?
Opposite: disgust-1-dislike


TRUST
-----
[trust-1-grateful] Grateful
Question: What are you thankful for? Who or what has contributed to your well-being?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to accept mistreatment or avoid expressing needs.
Tip: To balance, consider: What needs addressing despite gratitude? How can you appreciate what you have while still seeking growth?
Opposite: anger-2-mad

[trust-2-peaceful] Peaceful
Question: What brings you calm and tranquility? What helps you feel centered and at ease?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to avoid necessary conflicts or become complacent.
Tip: To balance, consider: What issues need to be addressed? How can you maintain peace while taking action when needed?
Opposite: anger-3-frustrated

[trust-3-accepted] Accepted
Question: Where do you feel you belong? Who accepts and values you as you are?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to conform too much or lose your authentic self.
Tip: To balance, consider: Are you being true to yourself? What parts of you need expression even if they differ from others?
Opposite: disgust-3-aweful

[trust-4-hopeful] Hopeful
Question: What are you hoping for? What positive outcome do you envision?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to wait passively instead of taking action.
Tip: To balance, consider: What action can you take toward your hopes? How can you be both hopeful and proactive?
Opposite: disgust-4-disapproval


FEAR
----
[fear-1-nervous] Nervous
Question: What uncertainty is creating unease? What feels unpredictable or risky right now?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to avoid opportunities or become paralyzed by worry.
Tip: To balance, consider: What small step could you take despite the nerves? What support or preparation might help?
Opposite: trust-2-peaceful

[fear-2-scared] Scared
Question: What feels threatening? What danger or harm are you sensing?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to see threats everywhere or withdraw from life.
Tip: To balance, consider: What evidence exists for this fear? What safety or support is available to you?
Opposite: anticipation-2-eager

[fear-3-anxious] Anxious
Question: What are you worried about? What uncertain outcome concerns you?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to ruminate excessively or create problems that don't exist.
Tip: To balance, consider: What can you control? What action would help address this concern?
Opposite: anticipation-3-interested

[fear-4-insecure] Insecure
Question: What makes you doubt yourself? What do you fear others might think or see?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to constantly seek validation or avoid putting yourself forward.
Tip: To balance, consider: What evidence supports your worth? What strengths do you have regardless of others' opinions?
Opposite: joy-2-confident


SURPRISE
--------
[surprise-1-startled] Startled
Question: What just happened that you didn't expect? What caught you off guard?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to become hypervigilant or jumpy.
Tip: To balance, consider: What can help you feel more grounded? How can you assess the situation calmly?
Opposite: anticipation-1-excited

[surprise-2-confused] Confused
Question: What doesn't make sense? What are you trying to understand?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to doubt everything or struggle to make decisions.
Tip: To balance, consider: What do you know for certain? Who or what could help clarify this?
Opposite: joy-1-optimistic

[surprise-3-amazed] Amazed
Question: What wonderful thing surprised you? What exceeds your expectations?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to overlook practical considerations or become easily impressed.
Tip: To balance, consider: What practical steps follow this amazement? How can you build on this wonder?
Opposite: sad-1-hurt

[surprise-4-disappointed] Disappointed
Question: What did you hope for that didn't happen? What expectation wasn't met?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to become cynical or stop hoping for positive outcomes.
Tip: To balance, consider: What can you learn from this? What new possibilities might exist?
Opposite: anticipation-4-stressed


SADNESS
-------
[sad-1-hurt] Hurt
Question: What caused this pain? What action or words wounded you?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to hold grudges or see harm in innocent actions.
Tip: To balance, consider: What might have been the intention? How can you communicate your hurt or seek healing?
Opposite: surprise-3-amazed

[sad-2-depressed] Depressed
Question: What feels heavy or hopeless? What has drained your energy or joy?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to withdraw completely or lose sight of any positive aspects.
Tip: To balance, consider: What small action could you take? Who could you reach out to for support?
Opposite: joy-1-optimistic

[sad-3-lonely] Lonely
Question: Who or what are you missing? What connection do you long for?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to push others away or believe no one cares.
Tip: To balance, consider: Who is available to you? What small step could you take toward connection?
Opposite: joy-3-joyful

[sad-4-ashamed] Ashamed
Question: What do you believe you did wrong? What part of yourself are you judging?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to hide your true self or believe you're fundamentally flawed.
Tip: To balance, consider: What would you tell a friend in this situation? How can you learn and grow from this?
Opposite: anger-1-aggressive


DISGUST
-------
[disgust-1-dislike] Dislike
Question: What doesn't align with your values? What feels wrong or distasteful?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to become judgmental or close-minded.
Tip: To balance, consider: What might you not understand yet? Can you create distance while staying open?
Opposite: joy-4-loving

[disgust-2-avoidance] Avoidance
Question: What are you trying to avoid? What feels too uncomfortable to face?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to miss opportunities or leave issues unresolved.
Tip: To balance, consider: What small step could you take toward this? What support might help you face it?
Opposite: fear-2-scared

[disgust-3-aweful] Awful
Question: What feels deeply wrong or distressing? What violates your sense of rightness?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to catastrophize or see negativity everywhere.
Tip: To balance, consider: What aspects are truly concerning? What can you do about it?
Opposite: trust-3-accepted

[disgust-4-disapproval] Disapproval
Question: What behavior or situation goes against your values? What do you judge as wrong?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to become rigid or alienate others.
Tip: To balance, consider: What circumstances might explain this? How can you hold standards while showing compassion?
Opposite: trust-4-hopeful


ANGER
-----
[anger-1-aggressive] Aggressive
Question: What feels threatening or intolerable? What are you ready to fight against?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to damage relationships or escalate conflicts.
Tip: To balance, consider: What outcome do you want? How can you stand firm while staying constructive?
Opposite: sad-4-ashamed

[anger-2-mad] Mad
Question: What boundary was crossed? What feels unjust or wrong?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to lash out or hold onto resentment.
Tip: To balance, consider: What do you need to feel better? How can you express this anger constructively?
Opposite: trust-1-grateful

[anger-3-frustrated] Frustrated
Question: What is blocking your progress? What obstacle keeps getting in your way?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to give up too quickly or become impatient with others.
Tip: To balance, consider: What different approach could you try? What is within your control?
Opposite: trust-2-peaceful

[anger-4-critical] Critical
Question: What could get in the way if this person does it wrong... or this situation is wrong?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to block innovation or shut people down.
Tip: To balance, consider: How big is the risk or transgression? What is going well or what is the person doing right?
Opposite: fear-4-insecure

Question: What are you looking forward to? What positive event energizes you?

Tip: To balance, consider: What preparation is needed? How can you sustain this energy wisely?
Opposite: surprise-1-startled

Question: What are you ready to pursue? What opportunity calls to you?

Tip: To balance, consider: What might you need to know first? How can you channel this eagerness effectively?
Opposite: fear-2-scared

[anticipation-3-interested] Interested
Question: What catches your attention? What do you want to learn or explore?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to become distracted or spread yourself too thin.
Tip: To balance, consider: What deserves your deepest focus? How can you explore while maintaining priorities?
Opposite: fear-3-anxious

[anticipation-4-stressed] Stressed
Question: What demands are weighing on you? What feels like too much to handle?
Risk: If you over-use this feeling, it could cause you to break down or make poor decisions under pressure.
Tip: To balance, consider: What can you delegate or postpone? How can you care for yourself during this time?
Opposite: surprise-4-disappointed
