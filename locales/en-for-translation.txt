# ===== FEAR EMOTIONS =====
EMOTION_FEAR_1_NAME: Nervous
EMOTION_FEAR_1_DESC: A state of unease or apprehension about something uncertain. Nervousness is a natural response to situations that feel challenging or unfamiliar.
EMOTION_FEAR_1_QUESTION: What is creating risk? What feels unpredictable or out of control?
EMOTION_FEAR_1_OVERLOAD_RISK: If you over-use this feeling, you may see danger everywhere, overthink, or become paralyzed by what-if scenarios.
EMOTION_FEAR_1_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, name one specific risk you are concerned about and one small action you can take to prepare or test your assumptions.
EMOTION_FEAR_1_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that something feels uncertain or risky and may need caution or preparation.
EMOTION_FEAR_1_FEELING_1: Threatened
EMOTION_FEAR_1_FEELING_2: Uneasy
EMOTION_FEAR_1_FEELING_3: Jittery
EMOTION_FEAR_1_BORDER_INFO: Nervous is near Trust because it involves vulnerability and the need for safety.

EMOTION_FEAR_2_NAME: Scared
EMOTION_FEAR_2_DESC: An intense feeling of fear or alarm. Being scared is your body's way of alerting you to potential danger.
EMOTION_FEAR_2_QUESTION: What or who do you care about that's at risk? What danger or harm are you sensing?
EMOTION_FEAR_2_OVERLOAD_RISK: If you over-use this feeling, you may withdraw from what matters most or avoid important steps because they feel too scary.
EMOTION_FEAR_2_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, clarify what you most want to protect and choose one action that increases safety while still honoring what you care about.
EMOTION_FEAR_2_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that something you care about feels threatened and needs protection or attention.
EMOTION_FEAR_2_FEELING_1: Frightened
EMOTION_FEAR_2_FEELING_2: Terrified
EMOTION_FEAR_2_FEELING_3: Alarmed

EMOTION_FEAR_3_NAME: Anxious
EMOTION_FEAR_3_DESC: A feeling of worry, nervousness, or unease about something with an uncertain outcome. Anxiety can be a signal to prepare or take action.
EMOTION_FEAR_3_QUESTION: What general threats or big uncertainties are present? What do you need to prepare for or protect against?
EMOTION_FEAR_3_OVERLOAD_RISK: If you over-use this feeling, you may ruminate, catastrophize, or create imagined problems that drain your energy.
EMOTION_FEAR_3_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, sort your worries into 'things I can influence' and 'things I cannot,' then choose one small step on something you can influence.
EMOTION_FEAR_3_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that you are scanning for long-term threats or uncertainties that may need planning or protection.
EMOTION_FEAR_3_FEELING_1: Dread
EMOTION_FEAR_3_FEELING_2: Worry
EMOTION_FEAR_3_FEELING_3: Tense

EMOTION_FEAR_4_NAME: Insecure
EMOTION_FEAR_4_DESC: A lack of confidence or certainty about yourself or your place in a situation. Insecurity often stems from self-doubt or fear of rejection.
EMOTION_FEAR_4_QUESTION: What makes you doubt yourself? What do you fear others might think or see?
EMOTION_FEAR_4_OVERLOAD_RISK: If you over-use this feeling, you may constantly seek validation, people-please, or hold back your contributions.
EMOTION_FEAR_4_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, remember concrete evidence of your strengths and seek grounded feedback from someone you trust, not just imagined criticism.
EMOTION_FEAR_4_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that your sense of worth, competence, or acceptance feels shaky and you may need reassurance, learning, or boundaries.
EMOTION_FEAR_4_FEELING_1: Rejected
EMOTION_FEAR_4_FEELING_2: Inadequate
EMOTION_FEAR_4_FEELING_3: Uncertain
EMOTION_FEAR_4_BORDER_INFO: Insecure is near Surprise because it involves unexpected threats to self-worth.

# ===== SURPRISE EMOTIONS =====
EMOTION_SURPRISE_1_NAME: Startled
EMOTION_SURPRISE_1_DESC: A sudden feeling of shock or alarm caused by something unexpected. Being startled is an immediate, automatic response to surprise.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_1_QUESTION: What sudden change or signal needs your attention right now?
EMOTION_SURPRISE_1_OVERLOAD_RISK: If you over-use this feeling, you may remain jumpy or hyper-alert, treating everything as an emergency.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_1_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, pause to look, breathe, and check what is actually happening now versus what you feared might be happening.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_1_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that something unexpected just happened and your attention needs to orient quickly.

EMOTION_SURPRISE_1_FEELING_2: Shocked
EMOTION_SURPRISE_1_FEELING_3: Stunned
EMOTION_SURPRISE_1_BORDER_INFO: Startled is near Fear because it involves a sudden sense of threat or danger.

EMOTION_SURPRISE_2_NAME: Confused

EMOTION_SURPRISE_2_QUESTION: What information is missing or unclear? What would help you make sense of this?
EMOTION_SURPRISE_2_OVERLOAD_RISK: If you over-use this feeling, you may get stuck in doubt, delay decisions, or question everything about yourself and others.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_2_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, turn confusion into a question: define one thing you want to understand and seek an answer, experiment, or conversation.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_2_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that your current understanding does not fit the facts and you may need new information or perspective.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_2_FEELING_1: Disillusioned
EMOTION_SURPRISE_2_FEELING_2: Perplexed
EMOTION_SURPRISE_2_FEELING_3: Bewildered

EMOTION_SURPRISE_3_NAME: Amazed
EMOTION_SURPRISE_3_DESC: A feeling of great wonder and astonishment. Amazement is a positive form of surprise that fills you with awe.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_3_QUESTION: What exceeded your expectations? What possibility does this open?
EMOTION_SURPRISE_3_OVERLOAD_RISK: If you over-use this feeling, you may idealize people or situations and miss real limits or risks.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_3_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, name what delights you and also one practical step or boundary that keeps you grounded while you explore this new possibility.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_3_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that something is far better, bigger, or more beautiful than you expected and your sense of possibility is expanding.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_3_FEELING_1: Astonished

EMOTION_SURPRISE_3_FEELING_3: Impressed

EMOTION_SURPRISE_4_QUESTION: What expectation wasn't met? What matters to you about this outcome?
EMOTION_SURPRISE_4_OVERLOAD_RISK: If you over-use this feeling, you may slide into cynicism, giving up on future efforts or relationships.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_4_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, name the expectation clearly, then decide whether to revise the goal, your strategy, or the timeline—and choose one next action.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_4_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that reality did not match your hopes and something you cared about feels diminished or lost.
EMOTION_SURPRISE_4_FEELING_1: Betrayed
EMOTION_SURPRISE_4_FEELING_2: Dismayed

# ===== SADNESS EMOTIONS =====

EMOTION_SADNESS_1_QUESTION: What value or relationship was impacted? What healing or repair would help?

EMOTION_SADNESS_3_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, identify one small, safe way to reach toward connection—a message, a call, a shared space, or naming your need to someone you trust.
EMOTION_SADNESS_3_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that your need for connection, belonging, or being seen is not being met.
EMOTION_SADNESS_3_FEELING_1: Abandoned

EMOTION_SADNESS_4_FEELING_1: Remorseful

# ===== DISGUST EMOTIONS =====

EMOTION_DISGUST_2_FEELING_1: Hesitant

EMOTION_DISGUST_2_FEELING_3: Reluctant

EMOTION_DISGUST_3_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, clarify what exactly is harmful, then choose one realistic action—personal, relational, or systemic—that moves even slightly toward repair.
EMOTION_DISGUST_3_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that something feels deeply wrong, harmful, or out of integrity with your core values.

EMOTION_DISGUST_4_QUESTION: What expectation wasn't met? What value is prompting this reaction, and what change do you hope for?
EMOTION_DISGUST_4_OVERLOAD_RISK: If you over-use this feeling, you may become rigid, overly critical, or alienating to others.
EMOTION_DISGUST_4_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, connect to the value underneath your disapproval and express it in a way that invites dialogue and improvement, not shame.
EMOTION_DISGUST_4_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that a behavior or outcome does not meet your standards or values and that you care about doing better.
EMOTION_DISGUST_4_FEELING_1: Loathing

# ===== ANGER EMOTIONS =====
EMOTION_ANGER_1_NAME: Aggressive
EMOTION_ANGER_1_DESC: A forceful and confrontational expression of anger. Aggression is often a response to feeling threatened or frustrated.
EMOTION_ANGER_1_QUESTION: What boundary needs to be protected? What matters enough that you feel compelled to act?
EMOTION_ANGER_1_OVERLOAD_RISK: If you over-use this feeling, you may lash out in ways that harm others, escalate conflict, or undermine your own goals.
EMOTION_ANGER_1_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, pause before acting, use the energy to choose clear and firm words or actions, and focus on the outcome you truly want.
EMOTION_ANGER_1_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that you perceive a serious threat, injustice, or violation and feel a strong drive to defend or protect.
EMOTION_ANGER_1_FEELING_1: Hostile
EMOTION_ANGER_1_FEELING_2: Provoked
EMOTION_ANGER_1_FEELING_3: Combative
EMOTION_ANGER_1_BORDER_INFO: Aggressive is near Disgust because it involves rejecting or pushing away what's unacceptable.

EMOTION_ANGER_2_NAME: Mad
EMOTION_ANGER_2_DESC: An intense feeling of displeasure or rage. Being mad signals that something has violated your boundaries or expectations.
EMOTION_ANGER_2_QUESTION: What value or expectation was violated? What do you need to express or repair?
EMOTION_ANGER_2_OVERLOAD_RISK: If you over-use this feeling, you may hold grudges, replay the story, or attack rather than seek resolution.
EMOTION_ANGER_2_OVERLOAD_TIP: To balance, name specifically what was not okay and decide whether you want to set a boundary, ask for change, or consciously let it go.
EMOTION_ANGER_2_ADAPTIVE_PURPOSE: Signals that something feels unfair or not okay and that you want change or acknowledgment.
EMOTION_ANGER_2_FEELING_1: Enraged
EMOTION_ANGER_2_FEELING_2: Furious
EMOTION_ANGER_2_FEELING_3: Livid

EMOTION_ANGER_3_NAME: Frustrated
EMOTION_ANGER_3_DESC: A feeling of upset or annoyance when unable to achieve something. Frustration arises from blocked goals or unmet needs.
EMOTION_ANGER_3_QUESTION: What's getting in the way of your goal? What shift—internal or external—would help you move forward?
